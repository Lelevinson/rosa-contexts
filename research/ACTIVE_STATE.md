## Verified development comparison

On 80 matched development cells per arm, scorer v2 gives **fixed loop 70/80, Forge 55/80, and stock executor with deconflicted prompt 47/80** (F: [independent trace recount](studies/forge_baseline_20261115/INDEPENDENT_CHECK.md); [Forge report](studies/forge_baseline_20261115/REPORT.md)). Requests are 486 / 643 / 472 and reported total tokens 1,676,502 / 2,215,689 / 3,281,423 respectively; Forge omitted usage for two requests, so its total is a lower bound. The fixed loop and stock executor are historical controls, not concurrent reruns. A1 is not the verbatim stock prompt; S0 was not run. Menu and finish schemas differ, and one Forge and two A1 changing-task failures have missed-trigger validity defects; frozen denominators remain intact. These results do not establish a held-out or causal claim.

The independent review found two of 25 Forge failures with malformed JSON in the string response; the interface's causal contribution is unproven. Two more had malformed keys or short names inside valid JSON. This supersedes any suggestion that only five Forge failures have been inspected (F: independent recount).
