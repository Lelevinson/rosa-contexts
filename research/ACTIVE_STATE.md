## Headroom diagnostic (`headroom_diagnostic_20261112`; [index row](STUDY_INDEX.md), [ledger](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/DECISION_LEDGER.md))
Development evidence only: six reused tasks, one fixture, qwen3.5:9b and gpt-6-luna. No novelty claim.
- **Stage 0, offline relabel of 58 traces ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT.md)).** The stock prompt forbids batching ("NEVER call multiple tools in parallel") in all three baseline arms, and arms B and C add a contradicting "use independent tool calls together" (F). So their `batch_reads` 0/12 does not measure willingness to batch. The prohibition itself was already known ([context §0](ROSA_RESEARCH_CONTEXT.md)); the new part is that B and C were not deconflicted. Requests above a derived floor in supported episodes: baseline 82–112%, deconflicted Qwen 29%, Luna 14% (F; support judgments R). Blind relabel agreement 85%, kappa 0.81.
- **1a, prompt fix ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT_STAGE1.md)).** 12 episodes per arm: A0 63 vs stock 74 requests (−15%), 10/12 vs 9/12 supported, native operations +25% (F). About 6 independent pairs (temperature 0).
- **1b, width probe (same report).** Every N up to 32 read in one request; Luna 3 and Qwen 4 requests (floor 3). Qwen N=32 broke the requested `clocks` key in 2/2 answers; wall time about 6× from N=4 to 32; `ros2 node list` once omitted a live node (F).
- **1c, Luna vs Qwen ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT_STAGE1C.md)).** 12 Luna A0 episodes: 45 requests vs Qwen 63, both 10/12 supported (F). Frozen rule: "gap exists" not met; "no gap" met on the literal wording, inconclusive on the stricter reading (both disclosed). The excess is not only a weak-model gap.
- **2a, offline replay ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT_STAGE2A.md)).** Learned speculative expansion at tau 0.7 removes 6/63 Qwen requests (9.5%); static top-1 removes 10 (15.9%); oracle ceiling about 22%. The frozen kill bar fires on (a) under 10% (by one request) and (b) no gain over static top-1 (F, replay only).
- **Stage 3, deeper dependent tasks ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT_STAGE3.md)).** Generated fixture, 12 tasks, arm A0: Qwen 82/96 excess over the floor (85.4%), Luna 23/91 (25.3%, on the threshold edge); excess 95–100% avoidable, none dependency-driven. “Room” over A0 only (F; synthetic).
- **Stage 3b, ordinary prompt control ([report](studies/headroom_diagnostic_20261112/REPORT_STAGE3B.md)).** Same tasks, arm A1 (three discovery-first stock messages replaced by one neutral line): Qwen 47/96 (49.0%), Luna 13/91 (14.3%, one request from the 15% line), supported-complete unchanged (24/24, 23/24). Remaining Qwen excess is repeats and recovery after short node names; Luna's is a first-request habit. A0 and A1 ran in different sessions; A1 changes three messages at once (F; development evidence).

## Closed directions (scoped; not impossibility proofs)
- **Deconflicted-prompt ordinary control:** completed and adopted as the strong ordinary control for later comparisons; it removes most serial excess and is not a contribution (baseline repair, context §0).
- **Width probe:** call count holds to N=32; remaining issues are native operations, wall time and format. No further width runs planned.
- **Learned speculative result expansion:** stopped by its frozen kill bar. Reopen only on a workload with task-independent follow-up pairs and large results.
- **AutoTool-family live admission:** blocked (above). Earlier rounds R1–28, CPAW, native-read and narration findings stand in the [index](STUDY_INDEX.md) and [decisions](decisions/README.md).

## Open directions (no additional work authorized)
Whether the weak model's residual waste (repeats, recovery after short node names) is the project's target; which of the three removed prompt messages matters (not ablated); an A0 re-run in the same session as A1; native operations and wall time as the metric; format reliability at width; a fixed one-line enrichment (`node_info` after `node_list`) as an engineering choice. These are context, not authorization.
